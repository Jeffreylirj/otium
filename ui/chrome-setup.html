ONE-TIME SETUP

Connect your regular Chrome

Otium needs the Chrome Companion to open and fill application tabs in your existing Chrome profile.

  1. In your regular Google Chrome, open chrome://extensions from the address bar.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and choose the extension folder inside your Otium project.
  3. Open Otium Chrome Companion from Chrome’s Extensions menu and click Connect this Chrome. Approve the requested site access if you want Otium to fill employer pages.
  4. For real jobs, sign in to Simplify Copilot in the same Chrome profile and check its selected resume.
  5. Return to Otium and click Try demo or Prepare applications.

Already installed Otium? Click its Reload icon in chrome://extensions and refresh this page.

For real jobs Otium starts Copilot where its button is available. Sign in to employer sites yourself when prompted. Review each draft before submitting it.
